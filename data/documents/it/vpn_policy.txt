1. WHAT THE VPN IS FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------
NovaConnect VPN provides an encrypted tunnel from a NovaTech laptop to
the NovaTech internal network. It is required whenever you are outside
a NovaTech office and need to reach an internal system.

You NEED the VPN for:
  - NovaPeople HR portal (people.novatech.internal)
  - NovaDesk IT helpdesk (helpdesk.novatech.internal)
  - NovaSpend expense portal (spend.novatech.internal)
  - Internal Git servers, Jenkins, staging and production environments
  - Internal wikis, file shares and databases
  - Anything with a .internal address

You do NOT need the VPN for:
  - NovaTech email and calendar
  - NovaChat messaging
  - Video conferencing
  - Any SaaS tool reached through NovaID single sign-on
Connecting to the VPN for these services only slows them down. Do not
leave the VPN connected all day out of habit.

Inside a NovaTech office, on the NovaCorp Wi-Fi or a wired port, the
VPN is not required and will refuse to connect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO CONNECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Client   : NovaConnect Client, version 4.2 or later
Gateway  : vpn.novatech.internal
Available from the Software Catalog (Company Portal icon). It is
pre-installed on all laptops issued after January 2025.

Connection steps:
  1. Make sure you are on a working internet connection.
  2. Open the NovaConnect Client from the system tray or menu bar.
  3. Confirm the gateway shows vpn.novatech.internal. Select the
     nearest region: IN-South (Bengaluru), IN-West (Pune) or
     IN-Central (Hyderabad).
  4. Click Connect.
  5. Enter your NovaID username and password (see NT-IT-002).
  6. Approve the push notification in the NovaAuth app. You have 60
     seconds before the request expires.
  7. The tray icon turns green when connected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SESSION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Maximum session length   : 12 hours, then re-authentication required
Idle disconnect          : 30 minutes with no traffic
Concurrent sessions      : 1 per user. A second connection disconnects
                           the first.
Tunnel mode              : Split tunnel. Internal traffic goes through
                           the tunnel; general internet traffic goes
                           out through your own connection.
Bandwidth                : Streaming video and large personal
                           downloads over the tunnel are throttled.

4. COMMON CONNECTION PROBLEMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
"Authentication failed"
    Almost always a password issue, not a VPN issue. Confirm you can
    sign in at id.novatech.internal. If your password recently expired,
    reset it first per NT-IT-002 Section 5.

"MFA timeout"
    The NovaAuth push expired. Click Connect again and approve within
    60 seconds. If no push arrives, open NovaAuth manually and use the
    6-digit code option.

"Gateway unreachable"
    Try a different region in the client. If all three regions fail,
    check whether your home internet is working, then raise a NovaDesk
    ticket. Widespread outages are posted on status.novatech.example.

"Connected but internal sites do not load"
    Flush DNS and reconnect. On Windows: ipconfig /flushdns. On macOS:
    sudo killall -HUP mDNSResponder. If still failing, this is a P2
    ticket on NovaDesk.

"VPN disconnects every few minutes"
    Usually unstable home Wi-Fi. Move closer to the router or use a
    wired connection. NT-HR-002 requires a minimum of 25 Mbps for WFH.

Escalation paths and response times for all of these are defined in
NT-IT-004 (IT Support and Helpdesk Policy). This document does not
define SLAs.

5. SECURITY CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 The VPN may only be used from a NovaTech-issued laptop.
5.2 Do not connect from a shared or public computer under any
    circumstances.
5.3 Public Wi-Fi (airport, cafe, hotel) is permitted only with the VPN
    connected before any work application is opened.
5.4 Do not share your screen showing internal systems in a personal or
    non-NovaTech meeting.
5.5 All VPN sessions are logged: username, timestamp, source IP,
    duration and destination systems. Logs are retained for 180 days
    and reviewed by Information Security.
5.6 Attempting to bypass the VPN by tunnelling, proxying or exposing
    internal services to the public internet is a serious policy
    violation and is referred to Information Security and HR.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TRAVEL AND LOCATION RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 VPN access is geo-restricted to India by default.
6.2 Employees travelling abroad on business must request temporary
    geo-unblocking at least 3 business days before departure, through
    NovaDesk (category: Network > International VPN Access). The
    request must include destination country, travel dates and the
    approving manager. Approval is granted for the travel dates only
    and expires automatically.
6.3 Personal international travel does not qualify for geo-unblocking.
    NovaTech does not permit working from outside India (see NT-HR-002
    Section 6.2).
6.4 VPN access from certain countries is blocked by policy regardless
    of approval. The current list is published on the Information
    Security wiki.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PERSONAL DEVICE ACCESS - NOVAPORTAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Where an employee genuinely needs access from a personal device (for
example, while awaiting a laptop replacement), the NovaPortal virtual
desktop is the only approved route. NovaPortal runs in a browser at
portal.novatech.example, requires NovaID plus MFA, and allows no
copy-paste, printing or file download to the local machine. Access is
granted in 7-day blocks by manager request on NovaDesk.
