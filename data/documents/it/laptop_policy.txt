This document covers what device you are issued, what you may install
on it, and what happens if it is lost or damaged. If your laptop has
simply stopped working and you need it fixed, raise a ticket as
described in NT-IT-004 (IT Support and Helpdesk Policy).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STANDARD DEVICE ALLOCATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Role family                 Standard issue
Business / Operations / HR  NovaBook 14 (16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD)
Engineering / Data          NovaBook Pro 16 (32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD)
Design                      NovaBook Pro 16 + colour-calibrated
                            external display
Field Sales                 NovaBook 14 + 4G data SIM

Every employee receives exactly one primary laptop. A second device is
issued only with Director-level approval and a documented business
reason (for example, a build machine or a device-testing role).

Accessories issued by default: power adapter, laptop bag, USB-C hub,
wired headset. Additional accessories (external monitor, keyboard,
mouse, docking station) are available on request through NovaDesk and
require manager approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. REFRESH CYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Laptops are refreshed every 36 months from the asset issue date. IT
raises the replacement automatically 30 days before the due date.
Early replacement is considered only where:
  - the device has had 3 or more hardware failures in 12 months, or
  - a documented performance requirement cannot be met by the current
    specification, approved by the Director of IT Infrastructure.

Devices returned at refresh are wiped and either redeployed as loaner
units or disposed of through a certified e-waste vendor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Employees do NOT have local administrator rights by default.

3.2 Approved software is installed from the NovaTech Software Catalog,
    available from the Company Portal icon on your desktop. Catalog
    installs are self-service and need no approval.

3.3 Software that is not in the Catalog requires a Software Request on
    NovaDesk (category: Software > New Request). The request must
    include the software name, version, vendor, business justification
    and licensing model. IT Security completes its review within 5
    business days.

3.4 Temporary administrator access may be granted for a fixed 72-hour
    window through NovaDesk (category: Access > Temporary Elevation),
    with manager approval. Elevation is logged and automatically
    revoked at the end of the window.

3.5 Prohibited at all times, with no exception process:
      - Cracked, pirated or unlicensed software
      - Torrent and peer-to-peer file sharing clients
      - Cryptocurrency mining software
      - Unapproved remote-access tools
      - Personal cloud sync clients (personal Google Drive, Dropbox,
        OneDrive) signed in with a personal account
      - Browser extensions that read page content, unless listed in
        the Catalog

3.6 Open-source libraries pulled into project code are governed by the
    Engineering Dependency Policy (NT-IT-009), not by this section.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Full-disk encryption is enabled on all laptops and must not be
    disabled.
4.2 The screen must lock automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity.
4.3 The NovaShield endpoint agent must remain running. Tickets
    reporting that "NovaShield is slowing my machine" should go to
    NovaDesk; the agent must not be uninstalled.
4.4 Operating system updates must be applied within 14 days of
    release. Devices more than 30 days behind are blocked from the
    corporate network automatically.
4.5 Company data must not be copied to personal USB drives. USB mass
    storage is blocked by default; an exception can be requested for a
    30-day period with Director approval.
4.6 Sign-in credentials for the device follow NT-IT-002 (Password
    Policy).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PERSONAL USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Reasonable personal use is permitted: personal email in a browser,
news, music streaming, online shopping in breaks. Employees should
have no expectation of privacy for anything stored on or transmitted
through a NovaTech device.

Personal devices must never be used to access internal NovaTech
systems. Employees needing access from a personal device should use
the NovaPortal virtual desktop described in NT-IT-003 (VPN Policy),
Section 7.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DAMAGE, LOSS AND THEFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Accidental damage
    First accidental damage incident in a 24-month period: fully
    covered by NovaTech, no employee contribution.
    Second incident in the same 24-month period: employee contributes
    INR 5,000 toward repair or replacement, deducted from salary with
    written consent.
    Third and subsequent incidents: reviewed case by case by the IT
    Asset Committee with the HR Business Partner.

6.2 Negligence
    Damage caused by demonstrable negligence (liquid spill on an
    unattended device at a non-work location, device left in a vehicle)
    is treated as a second incident from the outset.

6.3 Loss or theft
    Report immediately to the IT Service Desk on ext. 4444 and to
    itsecurity@novatech.example. IT will remotely lock and wipe the
    device. A police complaint (FIR) must be filed and a copy uploaded
    to the NovaDesk ticket within 48 hours. Replacement is issued
    within 3 business days of the FIR copy being received.

6.4 Do not submit laptop repair costs as an expense claim. Hardware
    repairs are paid centrally by IT. Expense claims for laptop repair
    will be rejected by Finance (see NT-FIN-001, Section 5).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. LOANER DEVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A loaner laptop can be collected from the IT Bar at any NovaTech
office. Loaners are issued for a maximum of 10 working days. Extension
requires a NovaDesk ticket update. Loaner devices carry the standard
image and do not have local admin rights under any circumstances.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. RETURN OF ASSETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
All IT assets must be returned to the IT Bar on or before the last
working day, including the laptop, adapter, bag, hub, headset, access
card and any accessories. An Asset Clearance record is generated in
NovaDesk and is a mandatory input to the final settlement processed by
Payroll. Unreturned assets are recovered at book value from the final
settlement.
