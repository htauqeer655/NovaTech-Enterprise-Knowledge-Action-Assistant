1. SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This standard applies to every NovaTech account: the NovaID single
sign-on identity, laptop sign-in, VPN, internal applications, cloud
consoles, databases and service accounts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PASSWORD CONSTRUCTION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Minimum length
      Standard user accounts     : 14 characters
      Privileged / admin accounts: 20 characters
      Service accounts           : 32 characters, randomly generated

2.2 Complexity
    Passwords must include at least three of the following four:
      - lowercase letters
      - uppercase letters
      - digits
      - symbols

2.3 Passwords must not contain:
      - your name, employee ID, or username
      - the word "novatech" in any casing or with substitutions
      - more than 3 repeated characters in a row
      - a sequence of 4 or more consecutive keyboard characters

2.4 Passphrases of 4 or more unrelated words are encouraged and
    satisfy the complexity requirement provided the total length is at
    least 16 characters.

2.5 Reuse
    The last 10 passwords cannot be reused. A password must be at
    least 1 day old before it can be changed again (this prevents
    cycling back to an old password).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ROTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard user accounts      : every 180 days
Privileged / admin accounts : every 90 days
Service accounts            : every 365 days, or immediately on the
                              exit of any engineer who held the secret
Shared kiosk accounts       : every 30 days

Reminder emails are sent 14, 7 and 2 days before expiry. If a password
expires, the account is not disabled; the next sign-in forces a change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 MFA is mandatory for all employees on NovaID.
4.2 The approved second factor is the NovaAuth mobile application
    (push approval or 6-digit code). Hardware security keys are issued
    to employees with privileged access.
4.3 SMS one-time passwords are NOT an approved second factor at
    NovaTech and have been disabled since v5.0 of this standard.
4.4 Lost or replaced phone: raise a NovaDesk ticket (category:
    Access > MFA Re-enrolment). Identity is verified by a video call
    with the Service Desk before re-enrolment. Expect 1 business day.
4.5 MFA prompts must never be approved if you did not initiate the
    sign-in. Report unexpected prompts to itsecurity@novatech.example
    immediately - this is the most common sign of a compromised
    password.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW TO RESET OR CHANGE YOUR PASSWORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Self-service reset (the normal route, available 24x7)
      Step 1 : Go to id.novatech.internal/reset
      Step 2 : Enter your NovaTech email address
      Step 3 : Approve the push notification in the NovaAuth app
      Step 4 : Set a new password meeting Section 2
      Step 5 : Sign out and back in on your laptop. If you are working
               from home, connect the NovaConnect VPN BEFORE signing
               out, so the laptop can pick up the new credentials.
      A self-service reset takes effect across all systems within
      5 minutes.

5.2 Routine change while signed in
      Windows: Ctrl+Alt+Del > Change a password
      macOS  : System Settings > Users & Groups > Change Password
      Either route syncs to NovaID automatically.

5.3 When self-service is not possible
      If you have lost access to NovaAuth, or your account is locked
      and the timer has not expired, contact the IT Service Desk on
      ext. 4444 or raise a NovaDesk ticket (category: Access >
      Password Reset). Identity verification by video call is
      mandatory. Passwords are never issued over email or chat, and
      the Service Desk will never ask you for your current password.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 An account locks after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts.
6.2 The lock releases automatically after 30 minutes.
6.3 Repeated lockouts (3 or more in 24 hours) trigger an automated
    review by Information Security, and the account may be suspended
    pending contact with the employee.
6.4 A locked account is not the same as a disabled account. Disabled
    accounts are handled only through the HR-initiated exit process.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. STORING AND SHARING PASSWORDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Use NovaVault, the approved enterprise password manager, available
    in the Software Catalog. Browser-saved passwords are permitted only
    for non-privileged internal applications.
7.2 Passwords must never be stored in plain text files, spreadsheets,
    chat messages, code repositories, CI variables or ticket comments.
7.3 Sharing a personal account password is prohibited without
    exception, including with managers and with IT staff.
7.4 Where a credential genuinely must be shared between people, a
    NovaVault shared folder must be used, with named members and an
    owner. Shared folders are reviewed quarterly.
7.5 Secrets committed to a repository must be rotated immediately and
    reported to itsecurity@novatech.example, even if the commit was
    reverted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. SUSPECTED COMPROMISE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
If you believe your password has been exposed - phishing link clicked,
unexpected MFA prompt, password typed into a suspicious site:
  1. Change your password immediately via id.novatech.internal/reset
  2. Email itsecurity@novatech.example with the subject "SUSPECTED
     CREDENTIAL COMPROMISE"
  3. Call ext. 4477 (Security Hotline) if it is outside business hours
There is no penalty for reporting. Late reporting is the risk, not the
mistake itself.
