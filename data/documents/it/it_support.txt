2. INCIDENT VERSUS REQUEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Incident (INC) : something that used to work has stopped working.
                 Laptop will not boot, VPN failing, printer offline,
                 application throwing errors.
Request  (REQ) : you want something new. New software, an accessory,
                 access to a system, a new mailbox.

Choosing the right type matters because SLAs differ. Requests do not
carry incident SLAs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRIORITY LEVELS AND SLA
--------------------------------------------------------------------
P1 - Critical
    Definition : Complete work stoppage for an individual with no
                 workaround, or an outage affecting 10+ people, or any
                 confirmed security incident.
    Examples   : Laptop completely dead. Production system down.
                 Suspected account compromise.
    Response   : 1 hour
    Resolution target : 4 hours

P2 - High
    Definition : Significant impairment with a partial workaround.
    Examples   : VPN connects but internal apps unreachable. Laptop
                 boots but constantly crashes. Cannot access a system
                 needed for today's deadline.
    Response   : 4 business hours
    Resolution target : 1 business day

P3 - Medium
    Definition : Minor impairment, work continues.
    Examples   : Slow performance. One application misbehaving.
                 Peripheral not working.
    Response   : 1 business day
    Resolution target : 3 business days

P4 - Low / Requests
    Definition : New software, accessories, access requests,
                 informational queries.
    Response   : 2 business days
    Resolution target : 10 business days

Priority is set by the Service Desk, not by the requester. If you
believe the priority is wrong, reply on the ticket with the business
impact and it will be reviewed.

4. WHAT TO DO IF YOUR LAPTOP STOPS WORKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the single most common query to the Service Desk. Follow this
sequence.

Step 1 - Try the 60-second checks
    - Hold the power button for 10 seconds, then power on again.
    - Remove all external devices (hub, monitor, USB) and retry.
    - Try a different power adapter and a different wall socket.
    - Check for a charging light. No light at all usually means an
      adapter or battery fault.

Step 2 - Raise the ticket
    If it is still not working, call ext. 4444. Do not wait for email.
    A completely dead laptop is a P1 incident; a laptop that boots but
    is unusable is P2.
    Provide: your asset tag (sticker on the underside of the device),
    your location, and what you already tried.

Step 3 - Get a loaner so you can keep working
    Collect a loaner laptop from the IT Bar. If you are working from
    home, IT will courier a loaner to your registered address within
    1 business day for P1 incidents. Loaners are issued for up to
    10 working days (see NT-IT-001, Section 7).

Step 4 - Attendance
    You are not marked absent because your laptop failed. Quote the
    ticket number when regularising attendance for that day under
    NT-HR-003, Part D.

Note: this section covers a device that has failed. Questions about
which laptop you are entitled to, refresh cycles, damage charges and
software installation rights are answered in NT-IT-001 (Laptop, Device
and Software Usage Policy), not here.

6. ESCALATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Level 1 : Reply on the ticket asking for an update.
Level 2 : If the SLA response target has been missed, use the
          "Escalate" button on the ticket in NovaDesk. This notifies
          the Shift Lead.
Level 3 : If the resolution target has been missed by more than 50%,
          email itsm@novatech.example with the ticket number. The IT
          Service Manager responds within 1 business day.
Level 4 : Unresolved service complaints go to the Director of IT
          Infrastructure via your own manager.

Security incidents are never escalated through this chain. Report them
directly to itsecurity@novatech.example or the Security Hotline on
ext. 4477.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. TICKET CLOSURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A resolved ticket stays in "Resolved" status for 3 business days. If
you do not respond, it closes automatically. A closed ticket cannot be
reopened after 7 calendar days; raise a new ticket referencing the old
number.

A satisfaction survey is sent on closure. Survey responses feed the
quarterly service review.
