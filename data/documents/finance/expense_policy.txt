IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE
This policy covers day-to-day business expenses incurred while working
from your usual city. Expenses incurred while on an approved business
TRIP away from your base city are covered by NT-FIN-002 (Travel
Policy), which has different and higher limits. The process for
submitting and getting paid for any claim is in NT-FIN-003
(Reimbursement Policy).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 - PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own.
2. The expense must have a clear business purpose.
3. The cheapest reasonable option should be chosen.
4. If you would be uncomfortable explaining the expense in an
   all-hands meeting, do not incur it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 - CATEGORY LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Client meals (in your base city)
    Limit: INR 800 per person per meal.
    Requires: names and companies of all attendees recorded in the
    claim. An itemised bill is mandatory. A card slip alone is not
    acceptable.
    Alcohol: not reimbursable in any local client meal.

2.2 Internal team meals
    Limit: INR 1,200 per person per occasion.
    Frequency: maximum one team meal per team per quarter.
    Approval: the team's manager, in advance, in writing.
    Team meals above 15 attendees require Director approval.

2.3 Local transport for business
    Limit: INR 1,500 per day total.
    Covers cabs and auto fares for client visits, inter-office travel
    and late-night departures after 21:30.
    Not covered: your normal daily commute between home and office.
    The commute is never reimbursable.

2.4 Mobile and phone
    Grades L1 to L3 : INR 700 per month
    Grades L4 to L5 : INR 1,500 per month
    Grade L6 and above / Field Sales : INR 2,500 per month
    Submit one claim per month with the operator bill in your name.

2.5 Professional membership and certification
    Limit: INR 30,000 per employee per calendar year. This is the same
    pool as the learning budget in NT-HR-004 and is not additional.
    Requires manager approval before the purchase. Confirmed employees
    only.

2.6 Client gifts
    Limit: INR 2,500 per recipient per year.
    Requires Director approval in advance, without exception.
    Cash, vouchers convertible to cash, and gifts to government
    officials are prohibited entirely.

2.7 Office supplies and small equipment
    Limit: INR 3,000 per claim.
    Anything IT-related (keyboards, mice, hubs, headsets, monitors,
    software licences) must be requested through NovaDesk instead, and
    will NOT be reimbursed as an expense. See NT-IT-001, Section 1.

2.8 Home internet
    Not claimable. Covered by the fixed monthly allowance of
    INR 1,200 paid with salary under NT-HR-002, Section 7.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 - PRE-APPROVAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Pre-approval on NovaSpend is required before incurring:
  - Any single expense over INR 10,000
  - Any client gift
  - Any team meal
  - Any professional certification or membership
  - Any expense in a category not listed in Section 2

Pre-approval is a separate NovaSpend workflow (Expenses >
Pre-Approval Request) and must be raised at least 3 business days in
advance. An expense over INR 10,000 submitted without a pre-approval
number will be rejected regardless of merit.

Approval authority:
  Up to INR 10,000        Reporting manager
  INR 10,001 to 50,000    Reporting manager + Department Head
  INR 50,001 to 200,000   Department Head + Controller
  Above INR 200,000       Chief Financial Officer

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 - MEAL LIMITS: LOCAL VERSUS TRAVEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This distinction causes the most claim rejections, so it is stated
explicitly.

If you are working in your base city and take a client to lunch, the
limit is INR 800 per person and you claim it as a Client Meal under
this policy.

If you are on an approved business trip outside your base city, your
own meals are covered by a per diem of INR 1,800 per day (domestic)
and you do NOT claim them per meal. A client meal hosted while
travelling is claimed separately at the travel limit. See NT-FIN-002,
Section 5.

You cannot claim both a per diem and individual meal bills for the
same day.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 - NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The following are never reimbursed:
  - Daily home-to-office commute
  - Traffic fines, parking fines and licence penalties
  - Personal entertainment, spa, gym memberships
  - Laptop or device repairs (paid centrally by IT, NT-IT-001 Sec 6.4)
  - Home internet bills (fixed allowance instead)
  - Personal software subscriptions
  - Alcohol at local client meals
  - Expenses of a spouse, partner or family member
  - Any expense already paid on a company corporate card

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6 - CORPORATE CARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Corporate cards are issued to employees at grade L4 and above with
Department Head approval. Card transactions must be reconciled in
NovaSpend within 15 calendar days of the statement date. Unreconciled
transactions after 45 days are recovered from salary. A card is not a
substitute for pre-approval; the same Section 3 thresholds apply.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7 - POLICY VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
First occurrence   : claim rejected, employee counselled by manager
Second occurrence  : claim rejected, formal note to HR Business Partner
Deliberate false claim : treated as misconduct and referred to HR
                         regardless of value
