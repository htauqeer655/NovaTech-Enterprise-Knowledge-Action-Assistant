--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHEN THIS POLICY APPLIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy applies from the moment you leave your base city on
approved business until you return. Expenses incurred inside your base
city, including local client meals and local cabs, are covered by
NT-FIN-001 (Business Expense Policy) with different limits.

Base city is the city recorded in NovaPeople as your work location:
Bengaluru, Pune or Hyderabad. Fully remote employees have their
registered home city recorded as base city.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. APPROVAL BEFORE YOU BOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Every trip begins with a Travel Request Form (TRF) in NovaSpend
    (Travel > New Travel Request). No TRF, no booking, no
    reimbursement.

2.2 Advance notice
      Domestic travel      : TRF at least 10 business days before
                             departure
      International travel : TRF at least 21 business days before
                             departure
    Late TRFs are accepted only with Department Head approval and may
    result in higher fares being charged to the team's budget.

2.3 Approval chain
      Domestic, under INR 50,000 total    : Reporting manager
      Domestic, INR 50,000 and above      : Manager + Department Head
      International, any value            : Manager + Department Head
                                            + Controller
      Travel to a restricted country      : Additionally, Chief
                                            Financial Officer

2.4 Once approved, the NovaTrip Desk books flights and hotels. Do not
    self-book. Self-booked travel is reimbursed only where the
    NovaTrip Desk confirms in writing that it could not fulfil the
    booking, or in a documented emergency.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FLIGHTS AND TRAINS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Domestic
    Economy class only, for all grades.
    Rail is required instead of air where the journey is under 5 hours
    by train and the fare difference exceeds INR 3,000. Rail class:
    AC 2-Tier, or AC Chair Car for day journeys.
    Book the lowest logical fare within a 2-hour window of the
    requested departure time.

3.2 International
    Economy class for flights with a total flying time up to 8 hours.
    Premium economy is permitted for flights over 8 hours for grade L5
    and above.
    Business class requires Chief Financial Officer approval and is
    granted only for flights over 12 hours for grade L7 and above.

3.3 Changes and cancellations
    Change fees are reimbursable where the change is business-driven.
    Change fees for personal reasons are borne by the employee.
    Cancellation must be notified to the NovaTrip Desk as soon as the
    trip is called off; no-show charges are recovered from the
    employee's team budget.

3.4 Airline loyalty points earned on business travel may be retained
    by the employee. Points must never influence the choice of carrier.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ACCOMMODATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Nightly room limits, inclusive of taxes:
    Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru,
    Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata)      INR 6,000
    Tier 2 and other Indian cities          INR 4,000
    International - Band A (USA, UK, EU,
    Singapore, Japan, Australia)            USD 180
    International - Band B (all others)     USD 110

Breakfast included in the room rate is preferred and does not count
against the per diem. Room service, mini-bar, laundry under 3 nights,
paid movies and spa charges are not reimbursable.

Where an employee stays with family or friends instead of a hotel, a
flat "stay-with-family" allowance of INR 1,000 per night is payable,
with no receipt required, up to 7 nights per trip.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MEALS AND PER DIEM WHILE TRAVELLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Your own meals while travelling are covered by a per diem. You do
    not submit individual meal bills for yourself.
      Domestic per diem             : INR 1,800 per day
      International Band A per diem : USD 75 per day
      International Band B per diem : USD 50 per day

5.2 Partial days
      Departure or return day with more than 8 hours of travel: full
      per diem.
      Less than 8 hours: 50 percent of the per diem.
      A day with no overnight stay away from base city: no per diem.
      Use the local expense limits in NT-FIN-001 instead.

5.3 If the company or a client pays for a meal, deduct from that day's
    per diem: breakfast 15 percent, lunch 35 percent, dinner 50 percent.

5.4 Hosting a client while travelling is NOT part of the per diem. It
    is claimed separately as a Client Meal - Travel, with a limit of
    INR 1,500 per person domestic, or USD 60 per person international.
    Attendee names and an itemised bill are mandatory.
    Note: the equivalent limit for a client meal in your own city is
    INR 800 per person (NT-FIN-001, Section 2.1). The two limits are
    different on purpose; use the one that matches where you were.

5.5 Alcohol is not reimbursable on per diem or on client meals, other
    than at a formal client dinner explicitly pre-approved by a
    Department Head, capped at INR 1,000 per attendee.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. GROUND TRANSPORT ON TRIP
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Airport transfers: app-based cab or airport taxi, actuals.
City travel at destination: actuals, up to INR 2,000 per day.
Car rental: permitted only where public transport and cabs are
impractical, with manager approval in the TRF.
Personal vehicle used for business travel: INR 12 per kilometre, with
a log of date, route and distance. Fuel bills are not claimed
separately when mileage is claimed.
Parking and tolls on business trips: actuals, receipts required.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. VISAS, INSURANCE AND HEALTH
--------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Visa fees, visa service charges, passport renewal where required
    for a business trip, and mandatory vaccinations are reimbursed at
    actuals with receipts.
7.2 NovaTech provides travel insurance for all approved business
    travel. The policy number and emergency assistance number are
    issued with the TRF approval. Employees must not purchase separate
    travel insurance; such claims are rejected.
7.3 Medical expenses abroad during business travel are handled by the
    travel insurer, not through NovaSpend.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WORKING WHILE TRAVELLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 A business travel day is a working day. It is not WFH and does not
    consume your monthly WFH allowance under NT-HR-002.
8.2 Mark attendance in NovaPeople as "Off-site" with the destination
    (NT-HR-003, Part A.3).
8.3 International travel requires a temporary VPN geo-unblock
    requested at least 3 business days before departure per NT-IT-003,
    Section 6.2. Request this at the same time as your TRF; travel
    approval does not automatically grant VPN access.
8.4 Working from another location for personal reasons is Work From
    Anywhere, not business travel, and is governed by NT-HR-002,
    Section 6.2. WFA is unpaid in terms of expenses: no per diem, no
    accommodation, no transport.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
9. HOW TO SUBMIT A TRAVEL EXPENSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 : Open NovaSpend > Travel > My Trips and select the trip.
Step 2 : Click "Create Expense Report from TRF". The trip details,
         approval chain and cost centre are pre-filled.
Step 3 : Add each expense line, choosing the category (Airfare, Hotel,
         Per Diem, Ground Transport, Client Meal - Travel, Visa,
         Other).
Step 4 : For Per Diem, enter the number of full and partial days.
         NovaSpend calculates the amount. No receipts are needed for
         per diem.
Step 5 : Attach receipts for every other line item above INR 500.
Step 6 : Submit. The report routes to your manager, then to Finance.
Step 7 : Track status under NovaSpend > My Claims.

The submission deadline, receipt rules, payment cycle and rejection
handling are defined in NT-FIN-003 (Reimbursement Policy) and apply to
travel claims exactly as they do to any other claim.
