This document explains HOW to get your money back. What you are
allowed to spend, and up to what limit, is in NT-FIN-001 and
NT-FIN-002. If your question is about a limit, this is the wrong
document.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHERE CLAIMS ARE SUBMITTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
All claims go through NovaSpend at spend.novatech.internal. NovaSpend
is an internal system, so you must be in the office or connected to
the NovaConnect VPN to reach it (NT-IT-003, Section 1).

Claims sent by email, chat or paper are not accepted and will not be
processed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DEADLINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Submit within 30 calendar days of the expense date.
2.2 Claims between 31 and 45 days old require a written justification
    in the claim and Department Head approval.
2.3 Claims older than 45 calendar days are not payable. NovaSpend
    blocks the submission. There is no appeal process for this.
2.4 For a multi-day business trip, the clock starts on the last day of
    the trip, not on the date of each individual receipt.
2.5 Claims for expenses in the previous financial year must be
    submitted by 10 April regardless of the 30-day rule.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR A CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Always required, for every claim:
      - Date of expense
      - Amount and currency
      - Expense category
      - Business purpose in one sentence
      - Cost centre (pre-filled from your NovaPeople profile)
      - Project code, if the expense is billable to a client

3.2 Receipts
      Above INR 500  : a legible receipt or invoice is mandatory
      Up to INR 500  : a self-declaration is accepted, limited to
                       3 self-declared items per claim and a total of
                       INR 1,500 per month
      Above INR 5,000: a GST-compliant tax invoice showing the NovaTech
                       entity name, address and GSTIN is mandatory.
                       A simple receipt is not sufficient.

3.3 Category-specific documents
      Client meal        : itemised bill plus attendee names and
                           companies (card slip alone is rejected)
      Team meal          : itemised bill plus the written manager
                           approval obtained in advance
      Local cab          : app receipt showing pickup, drop and fare
      Mobile bill        : operator invoice in the employee's own name
      Air travel         : e-ticket plus boarding passes for all
                           sectors, plus the airline invoice
      Hotel              : final folio (not the booking confirmation)
      Per diem           : no receipt required, but trip dates must
                           match the approved TRF
      Mileage            : distance log with date, route and purpose
      Certification      : invoice plus proof of completion or
                           enrolment
      Any expense above
      INR 10,000         : the pre-approval number from NovaSpend

3.4 File format: PDF, JPG or PNG. Maximum 10 MB per file. Photographs
    of receipts are acceptable if all four corners and the total are
    readable. Screenshots of a payment app alone are not acceptable as
    a receipt.

3.5 Foreign currency: attach the card statement or forex slip showing
    the actual rate charged. If none is attached, NovaSpend applies
    the RBI reference rate for the transaction date.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. APPROVAL AND PROCESSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage 1 : Manager approval. Target 3 business days. Auto-reminder at
          day 3, auto-escalation to skip-level at day 5.
Stage 2 : Finance verification. Target 4 business days. Finance checks
          policy limits, receipts and duplicates.
Stage 3 : Payment queue.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PAYMENT CYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Reimbursements are paid once a month, with salary, on the last
    working day of the month.
5.2 To be included in a given month's payout, a claim must be fully
    approved by Finance on or before the 25th of that month.
5.3 A claim approved on the 26th or later is paid in the following
    month's cycle.
5.4 Payment is made to the same bank account registered for salary.
    Bank details are changed through NovaPeople, not NovaSpend, and a
    change made after the 20th applies from the next cycle.
5.5 Out-of-cycle payment is available only for claims above
    INR 50,000, on Controller approval, and is processed within
    5 business days of that approval.
5.6 Reimbursements are not taxable income and appear as a separate
    line on the payslip, not in gross earnings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT NOT TO SUBMIT HERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
These are paid through other routes and will be rejected in NovaSpend:
  - Home internet - fixed allowance with salary (NT-HR-002, Sec 7)
  - Home office setup for fully remote employees - one-time payment
    through Payroll, initiated by HR
  - Laptop or accessory purchases - requested through NovaDesk
    (NT-IT-001, Sec 1)
  - Laptop repairs - paid centrally by IT (NT-IT-001, Sec 6.4)
  - Travel insurance - arranged by the company (NT-FIN-002, Sec 7.2)
  - Relocation costs - separate process owned by HR (NT-HR-011)
  - Leave encashment - processed in final settlement by Payroll

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. REJECTED CLAIMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 A rejected claim shows the reason in NovaSpend and triggers an
    email.
7.2 You may correct and resubmit within 7 calendar days of the
    rejection. A resubmission within this window keeps the original
    submission date for the purposes of the 30-day and 45-day
    deadlines in Section 2.
7.3 After 7 calendar days the claim is closed and a fresh claim is
    required, subject to the normal deadlines.
7.4 Most common rejection reasons, in order:
      1. Missing or illegible receipt
      2. Amount exceeds the category limit
      3. Wrong policy applied (local meal limit claimed while on
         travel, or travel per diem claimed for a day in base city)
      4. Missing pre-approval number for an expense above INR 10,000
      5. Card slip submitted instead of an itemised bill
      6. Duplicate of a claim already submitted

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. TRACKING AND QUERIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
NovaSpend > My Claims shows one of these statuses:
  Draft, Pending Manager, Pending Finance, Approved - Queued for
  Payment, Paid, Rejected, Closed.

If a claim is stuck in Pending Manager for more than 5 business days,
use the "Nudge" button before contacting Finance. Finance cannot
approve on a manager's behalf.
