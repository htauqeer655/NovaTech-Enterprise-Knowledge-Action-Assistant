1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------
NovaTech operates a hybrid working model. The office is the default
place of work, and Work From Home (WFH) is an approved flexibility,
not an entitlement. This policy describes how many WFH days employees
get, how to request them, and the conditions attached.

WFH is NOT leave. On a WFH day the employee is fully working and is
expected to be available, contactable and productive.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW MANY WFH DAYS DO I GET?
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Confirmed employees      : 8 WFH days per calendar month
Employees on probation   : 4 WFH days per calendar month, and only
                           after completing the first 60 days of
                           employment. During the first 60 days, WFH
                           is not permitted except under Section 6.
Interns                  : Not eligible for WFH.

WFH days do not carry over. Unused WFH days in January do not increase
the February allowance.

A maximum of 3 consecutive WFH days may be taken without additional
approval. 4 or more consecutive WFH days require HR Business Partner
approval in addition to manager approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ANCHOR DAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Every team has two "anchor days" each week on which all team members
are expected in the office. Anchor days are set by the reporting
manager and published on the team's NovaPeople calendar.

Company-wide anchor day: the first Wednesday of every month is an
in-office day for all employees at all locations (All-Hands day).
WFH is not approved on this day except under Section 6.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO REQUEST WFH
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 : Open NovaPeople (people.novatech.internal)
Step 2 : Choose "Attendance" then "Request Work From Home"
Step 3 : Select the date(s) and give a one-line reason
Step 4 : Submit at least 24 hours before the WFH day

Requests submitted less than 24 hours in advance are treated as
"Unplanned WFH" and require the manager to approve them in writing on
the same day. More than 2 Unplanned WFH days in a month will be
flagged to the HR Business Partner.

Manager approval is recorded in NovaPeople. A verbal "yes" from a
manager is not a valid approval and will show as an unapproved absence
in the monthly attendance report.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. EXPECTATIONS WHILE WORKING FROM HOME
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Be reachable on NovaChat and by phone during core hours
  (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST).
- Mark your attendance in NovaPeople by clicking "Start Day" and
  "End Day". Badge swipe obviously does not apply. Attendance rules for
  WFH days are described in NT-HR-003 (Attendance Policy).
- Join all scheduled meetings with camera on where the meeting
  organiser has requested it.
- Use the NovaConnect VPN for any access to internal systems. See
  NT-IT-003 (VPN Policy).
- Work from a location in India with a stable internet connection of
  at least 25 Mbps download.
- Company data must not be accessed on personal devices. See
  NT-IT-001 (Laptop and Device Policy).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. EXCEPTIONS AND SPECIAL SITUATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Medical WFH
    Employees with a medical condition, recent surgery, or pregnancy
    may be approved for extended WFH of up to 60 calendar days with a
    doctor's note submitted to the HR Business Partner. Medical WFH
    does not count against the monthly 8-day or 4-day allowance, and
    is available during probation.

6.2 Work From Anywhere (WFA)
    Once per leave year, an employee may work from any location in
    India for up to 20 consecutive working days. WFA requires:
      - 21 calendar days advance notice
      - Reporting manager approval
      - Director-level (L5+) approval
      - Confirmation that the employee is not on probation
    Working from outside India is not permitted under this policy for
    tax and data-residency reasons. Short business trips abroad are
    covered separately by NT-FIN-002 (Travel Policy) and are not WFA.

6.3 Emergency / Force Majeure
    In the event of a declared city-level disruption (flooding,
    transport strike, public health order), the Site Leader may declare
    a company-wide WFH day. These days do NOT count against the monthly
    allowance.

6.4 Weather or Local Disruption at Individual Level
    If an employee cannot reach the office due to a local issue, they
    may take an Unplanned WFH day. If they cannot work at all, it is
    leave, not WFH. See NT-HR-001 (Leave Policy).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. HOME OFFICE SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Employees working from home receive an internet allowance of
INR 1,200 per month. This is paid automatically with salary for
confirmed employees and does NOT require an expense claim. Do not
submit internet bills to NovaSpend; such claims will be rejected.
See NT-FIN-003 (Reimbursement Policy), Section 6.

A one-time home office setup allowance of INR 15,000 is available to
employees designated as Fully Remote in their offer letter. Hybrid
employees are not eligible for this allowance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WITHDRAWAL OF WFH
--------------------------------------------------------------------
WFH privileges may be temporarily withdrawn by the reporting manager
where there is a documented performance concern, a project-critical
phase, or repeated unavailability during core hours. Withdrawal must
be communicated in writing with a defined review date, and a copy sent
to the HR Business Partner.
