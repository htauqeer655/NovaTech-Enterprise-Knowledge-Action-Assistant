PART A - HOW ATTENDANCE IS RECORDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A.1  In the office
     Attendance is captured by the access-card reader at the building
     turnstile. Employees must swipe IN on arrival and swipe OUT on
     departure. Swipe data syncs to NovaPeople every 30 minutes.

A.2  Working from home
     On an approved WFH day, the employee must click "Start Day" and
     "End Day" in the NovaPeople Attendance tab. A WFH day with no
     Start Day entry is recorded as Absent, even if the WFH request
     itself was approved.

A.3  Client site or off-site
     Employees working at a client location must select
     "Attendance > Off-site" in NovaPeople and enter the client name.
     This requires manager confirmation within 5 working days.

A.4  What counts as a present day
     A day is marked Present when total recorded hours are 8 hours or
     more, or when the employee has an approved leave, holiday or
     approved WFH record for that date.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
PART B - EXPECTED HOURS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard working day  : 8 hours of work + 1 hour break
Standard window       : 09:30 to 18:30 IST
Flexible start        : any time between 08:00 and 11:00
Core hours            : 11:00 to 16:00 IST - attendance is mandatory
Weekly average        : 40 hours across Monday to Friday

Full details of shifts, flexible timing and the holiday calendar are
in NT-GEN-001 (Working Hours and Holidays). This document covers only
the recording and correction of attendance.

PART C - LATE MARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
C.1  A Late Mark is applied when the first swipe-in (or Start Day
     click) is after 11:00 AM and no prior intimation was given to the
     reporting manager.

C.2  3 Late Marks in a calendar month result in a deduction of one
     half-day of Annual Leave.

C.3  6 Late Marks in a calendar month trigger a documented
     conversation with the reporting manager and a note to the HR
     Business Partner.

C.4  Late Marks reset to zero on the 1st of each month.

C.5  A Late Mark is not applied if the employee informs the manager
     before 11:00 AM, if the employee is on an approved client visit,
     or if the delay is due to a company-arranged cab that was late
     (transport ticket number required).

PART D - MISSED PUNCH AND REGULARISATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This section answers the common question: "What happens if I forget to
mark my attendance?"

D.1  A missed punch is any day with a missing swipe-in, a missing
     swipe-out, or a missing Start Day / End Day entry on a WFH day.

D.2  Nothing is deducted immediately. The day appears in NovaPeople
     as "Attendance Exception" and must be regularised.

D.3  To regularise:
       1. Open NovaPeople > Attendance > My Exceptions
       2. Select the date showing the exception
       3. Choose a reason: Forgot to Swipe / Card Not Working /
          System Error / Off-site Work / Other
       4. Enter the actual in and out times
       5. Submit for manager approval

D.4  Deadline: regularisation must be submitted within 5 working days
     of the exception date. After 5 working days the NovaPeople form
     locks and the day is converted to Loss of Pay.

D.5  Limit: a maximum of 3 regularisations are allowed per calendar
     month. The 4th and subsequent requests in the same month require
     HR Business Partner approval in addition to manager approval.

D.6  If the form has already locked, email
     attendance@novatech.example with the date, the reason and your
     manager in copy. The Attendance Cell may reopen a locked entry
     once per quarter per employee, at its discretion.

D.7  If the access card itself is faulty, raise an IT ticket on
     NovaDesk (category: Access Card) to get it replaced. The ticket
     number should be quoted in the regularisation reason. Card
     replacement is handled by IT, not by HR. See NT-IT-004
     (IT Support and Helpdesk).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
PART E - ABSENCE WITHOUT INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
E.1  An absence with no leave request and no intimation is recorded as
     Unauthorised Absence and is Loss of Pay for that day.

E.2  2 consecutive Unauthorised Absences trigger an automated email
     to the employee and the reporting manager.

E.3  5 consecutive Unauthorised Absences with no contact are treated
     as abandonment of employment and are referred to the HR Business
     Partner for formal action.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
PART F - MONTHLY CYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Attendance month runs from the 21st of one month to the 20th of the
next. The attendance register freezes at 23:59 on the 20th and is sent
to Payroll on the 21st. Any correction after the freeze can only be
adjusted in the following month's payroll.
