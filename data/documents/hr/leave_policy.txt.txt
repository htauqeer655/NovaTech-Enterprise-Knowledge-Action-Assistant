SECTION 1 - PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This policy defines the types of leave available to NovaTech employees,
how leave is accrued, how it is approved, and what happens to unused
leave. It applies to all full-time employees. Interns and contractors
are covered under a separate agreement (NT-HR-014) and are NOT covered
by this document.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 - THE LEAVE YEAR
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The NovaTech leave year runs from 01 January to 31 December. This is
the same as the NovaTech financial reporting year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 - LEAVE TYPES AND ENTITLEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Annual Leave (also called Earned Leave)
    Confirmed employees receive 24 days of Annual Leave per leave year.
    Annual Leave accrues at 2 days per completed calendar month.
    Annual Leave is credited on the 1st of each month.

3.2 Sick Leave
    All employees receive 12 days of Sick Leave per leave year.
    Sick Leave is credited in full on 01 January (or on the joining
    date, pro-rated, for new joiners).
    A medical certificate is required for any Sick Leave absence of
    3 or more consecutive days.
    Sick Leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed.

3.3 Optional Holidays
    Every employee may take 2 Optional Holidays per year from the
    published Optional Holiday list. See NT-GEN-001 (Working Hours and
    Holidays) for the list of dates. Optional Holidays must be applied
    for at least 3 working days in advance.

3.4 Maternity Leave
    26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children.
    12 weeks for the third child onwards.
    Must be notified to the HR Business Partner at least 8 weeks before
    the expected start date.

3.5 Paternity Leave
    15 working days, to be used within 6 months of the child's birth.
    May be taken in a maximum of 3 separate blocks.

3.6 Bereavement Leave
    5 working days for the death of an immediate family member
    (spouse, parent, child, sibling, parent-in-law).
    2 working days for extended family.
    No documentation is required. Notification by message to the
    reporting manager is sufficient.

3.7 Marriage Leave
    5 working days, available once during the employee's tenure at
    NovaTech. Requires 30 days advance notice.

3.8 Leave Without Pay (LWP)
    Granted only after all Annual Leave is exhausted.
    Requires approval from the reporting manager AND the HR Business
    Partner. Maximum 20 working days in a leave year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 - LEAVE DURING PROBATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Employees serving probation accrue Annual Leave at the normal rate of
2 days per month, but may USE a maximum of 6 days of Annual Leave
during the entire probation period. Accrued but unused days are
released for use upon confirmation.

Sick Leave is available in full from day one and is NOT restricted
during probation.

Full details of probation terms are in NT-HR-004 (Probation Policy).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 - APPLYING FOR LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
All leave is applied for on the NovaPeople portal
(people.novatech.internal). Email or verbal requests are not valid
leave applications.

Required advance notice:
  1 to 2 days of leave        - 2 working days notice
  3 to 5 days of leave        - 7 calendar days notice
  6 to 10 days of leave       - 14 calendar days notice
  More than 10 days of leave  - 30 calendar days notice

Approval chain:
  Up to 5 consecutive days    - Reporting manager only
  6 to 10 consecutive days    - Reporting manager + HR Business Partner
  More than 10 days           - Reporting manager + HR Business Partner
                                + skip-level manager

Managers must action a leave request within 3 working days. If no
action is taken within 5 working days, the request is auto-escalated
to the skip-level manager on NovaPeople.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6 - EXCEPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Emergency Leave
    Where advance notice is impossible (accident, hospitalisation,
    family emergency), the employee must inform the reporting manager
    by phone or message before 11:00 AM on the first day of absence,
    and must file the leave request on NovaPeople within 3 working days
    of returning.

6.2 Blackout Periods
    Annual Leave of more than 3 consecutive days is not normally
    approved during:
      - The last 5 working days of any quarter (Finance and Sales only)
      - A declared production freeze window (Engineering only)
    Exceptions require skip-level manager approval.

6.3 Half-Day Leave
    Annual Leave and Sick Leave may be taken in half-day units.
    First half = 09:30 to 13:30. Second half = 14:30 to 18:30.
    A maximum of 4 half-days may be taken in a single calendar month.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7 - CARRY FORWARD AND ENCASHMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A maximum of 10 unused Annual Leave days may be carried forward into
the next leave year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or
they lapse with no compensation.

Annual Leave cannot be encashed while employed. On resignation or
termination, up to 15 days of accrued unused Annual Leave are encashed
at basic salary rate in the final settlement.
