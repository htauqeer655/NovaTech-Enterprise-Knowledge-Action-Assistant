1. SCOPE AND DURATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 All new employees of NovaTech Solutions serve a probation period
    of 6 months from the date of joining.

1.2 Probation may be extended ONCE, by a maximum of 3 months. An
    extension must be recommended by the reporting manager with written
    reasons and approved by the HR Business Partner at least 15 days
    before the original probation end date.

1.3 Employees rejoining NovaTech within 12 months of a previous exit
    serve a reduced probation of 3 months.

1.4 Internal transfers between teams do not restart probation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE CONFIRMATION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage 1 - Day 30 check-in
    Informal conversation between the employee and the reporting
    manager. No form, no rating. Notes optional.

Stage 2 - Day 90 review
    A formal written review on NovaPeople. The manager records progress
    against the onboarding goals agreed in the first two weeks. A
    rating of "Below Expectations" at this stage triggers a mandatory
    30-day improvement plan.

Stage 3 - Day 150 confirmation recommendation
    The manager submits one of three recommendations on NovaPeople:
      (a) Confirm
      (b) Extend probation by 3 months
      (c) Do not confirm
    HR Business Partner reviews and approves within 10 working days.

Stage 4 - Confirmation letter
    On approval, the confirmation letter is issued through NovaPeople
    within 5 working days of the probation end date. The letter is the
    official record; a verbal confirmation from a manager is not
    sufficient to change entitlements.

If no recommendation is submitted by the probation end date, the
employee is deemed confirmed automatically on the 7th calendar day
after the probation end date.

4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Can I work from home during probation?
A: Yes, but with limits. You cannot work from home during your first
   60 days. From day 61 until confirmation, you may take up to 4 WFH
   days per calendar month with manager approval. Anchor days and the
   monthly All-Hands day still apply. Medical WFH (NT-HR-002 Section
   6.1) is an exception and is available from day one.

Q: Can I take leave during probation?
A: Yes. You accrue 2 days of Annual Leave per month like everyone
   else, but you may use a maximum of 6 of those days before
   confirmation. Sick Leave is not restricted.

Q: Does my accrued leave disappear if I do not use it during
   probation?
A: No. Accrued but unused days remain in your balance and become
   available to use once you are confirmed.

Q: Can I claim the learning budget during probation?
A: No. The INR 30,000 annual learning budget starts from the
   confirmation date and is pro-rated for the remainder of that
   calendar year.

Q: Can I be sent on business travel during probation?
A: Yes, if the business requires it. The same travel policy applies
   (NT-FIN-002). No separate approval tier applies to probationers.

Q: What is my notice period if I resign during probation?
A: 15 calendar days. After confirmation it becomes 60 calendar days.

Q: Is my probation automatically extended if my manager is on leave?
A: No. If no recommendation is recorded by the probation end date, you
   are deemed confirmed on the 7th calendar day afterwards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. EXIT DURING PROBATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Either party may end the employment during probation with 15
    calendar days notice or payment in lieu.
5.2 Where employment is ended by NovaTech during probation, the
    employee receives encashment of accrued unused Annual Leave up to
    a maximum of 15 days, per NT-HR-001 Section 7.
5.3 All company assets must be returned on or before the last working
    day as per NT-IT-001 (Laptop and Device Policy), Section 8.
