PART 1 - STANDARD WORKING HOURS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Working week      : Monday to Friday
Weekly off        : Saturday and Sunday
Standard hours    : 09:30 to 18:30 IST
Working duration  : 8 hours of work plus a 1-hour break
Weekly commitment : 40 hours

Flexible start    : Employees may start any time between 08:00 and
                    11:00 and finish 9 hours later.
                    Start 08:00 -> finish 17:00
                    Start 09:30 -> finish 18:30
                    Start 11:00 -> finish 20:00

Core hours        : 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Every employee must be
                    available during core hours, whether in the office
                    or working from home. Meetings are scheduled inside
                    core hours wherever possible.

Lunch             : 1 hour, normally between 13:00 and 14:30. Not
                    counted as working time.
Short breaks      : Two 15-minute breaks are counted as working time.

Attendance recording rules, late marks and how to correct a missed
punch are in NT-HR-003 (Attendance Policy). This document defines the
hours themselves.

PART 2 - SHIFTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Some teams operate shifts to support global customers.

Shift A (India)   : 09:30 to 18:30 IST  - default for most teams
Shift B (Europe)  : 12:30 to 21:30 IST  - shift allowance INR 250/day
Shift C (Americas): 18:00 to 03:00 IST  - shift allowance INR 600/day
                                          plus company-provided cab
                                          both ways
Shift D (Early)   : 06:30 to 15:30 IST  - shift allowance INR 250/day

Shift rules:
  - Shift rosters are published at least 14 calendar days in advance.
  - No employee may be rostered on Shift C for more than 90
    consecutive calendar days without a 30-day break.
  - Employees on Shift C are not required to attend the monthly
    All-Hands in person.
  - A change of shift with less than 14 days notice requires the
    employee's written consent.
  - Shift allowances are paid with salary automatically and are NOT
    claimed through NovaSpend.

PART 3 - ON-CALL AND OVERTIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 On-call rotation applies to Engineering and IT Operations only.
    On-call allowance: INR 1,000 per weekday night, INR 2,000 per
    weekend day or public holiday.
3.2 An engineer who works more than 4 hours during an on-call incident
    outside working hours may take compensatory time off of half a day
    within the next 10 working days, with manager approval.
3.3 NovaTech does not operate a general paid-overtime scheme for
    salaried employees. Sustained work beyond 45 hours a week must be
    raised by the manager to the Department Head.

  2.  Monday      26 January 2026   Republic Day
  3.  Wednesday   04 March 2026     Holi
  4.  Friday      03 April 2026     Good Friday
  5.  Friday      01 May 2026       Labour Day
  6.  Wednesday   15 July 2026      Founders Day (NovaTech holiday)

  8.  Friday      02 October 2026   Gandhi Jayanti
  9.  Tuesday     10 November 2026  Diwali

provide a substitute weekday off for a public holiday falling on a
weekend. Only 9 of the 10 fixed holidays are therefore working-day
holidays in 2026.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Each employee may select 2 days from the list below. These are
separate from Annual Leave and do not reduce the 24-day Annual Leave
balance described in NT-HR-001.

  - Friday      16 January 2026   Pongal / Makar Sankranti
  - Thursday    19 March 2026     Ugadi / Gudi Padwa
  - Monday      30 March 2026     Eid al-Fitr
  - Tuesday     26 May 2026       Buddha Purnima
  - Thursday    06 June 2026      Eid al-Adha
  - Friday      28 August 2026    Onam
  - Friday      04 September 2026 Janmashtami
  - Monday      21 September 2026 Ganesh Chaturthi
  - Tuesday     20 October 2026   Dussehra
  - Tuesday     24 November 2026  Guru Nanak Jayanti

Rules for Optional Holidays:
  - Apply on NovaPeople at least 3 working days in advance.
  - Unused Optional Holidays lapse on 31 December and are not
    encashed or carried forward.
  - Optional Holidays cannot be combined to take more than 2 days.
  - Teams must maintain a minimum of 50 percent coverage on any
    Optional Holiday date; approval is first-come, first-served in
    NovaPeople.

PART 7 - QUICK ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What are the company's working hours?
A: 09:30 to 18:30 IST, Monday to Friday, with flexible start between
   08:00 and 11:00 and mandatory core hours from 11:00 to 16:00.

Q: How many holidays do we get in 2026?
A: 10 fixed public holidays plus 2 Optional Holidays of your choice.
   One fixed holiday (15 August) falls on a Saturday and is not
   substituted.

Q: Are Saturdays working days?
A: No. Monday to Friday only. The Bengaluru building is open on
   Saturday for employees who choose to come in, but it is not a
   working day and no attendance is expected.

Q: Do working hours change when I work from home?
A: No. The same hours and the same core hours apply. What changes is
   how attendance is recorded. See NT-HR-002 and NT-HR-003.

Q: Is a public holiday deducted from my leave balance?
A: No. Public holidays and Optional Holidays are separate from the
   24 days of Annual Leave.
